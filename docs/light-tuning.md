# How much oil the deep gives back

Generated by `npm run tuning` from the same code the game runs. Every number is a simulation with bots, not a measurement of players.

## The question

A flask holds 80 light and a descent may start with up to five. Exploring one of the big first floors takes about 130 steps, and a creature's blow drinks a few light more, so a floor costs roughly 130-170 light and the floors get dearer with depth. If the deep gave nothing back, two flasks would end on floor 2 or 3 and the boss on floor 7 could not be reached at all. If it gave too much, flasks would stop mattering and a descent would never end.

Light comes back from four places: oil jars lying on the floor, broken props, defeated creatures (new) and level-ups (+10 each). Props and creatures give oil more often the lower the lantern is, so a delver running dry is helped and one with a full lantern is not.

## How it was measured

- **Diver:** a bot that only wants to go down. It fights what is in its way, picks up loot within four steps of its path, takes every stairs at once and never goes home, so it stops only when the light is gone. Where it stops is how far that oil carries a player who does not collect. The *newcomer* finds the stairs by exploring; the *expert* already knows where they are. Both dodge marked tiles; the *sloppy* one does not.
- **Collector:** the bot behind the economy report. It gathers loot and leaves when the walk home would use up its reserve. Its gold per cent spent shows how much the extra oil inflates the economy (the simulated crowd is recalibrated to it).
- 40 descents per cell, the same seeds for every scenario.

### What each scenario means

- **No creature oil.** Creatures drop no oil; jars and props as chosen.
- **Flat prop oil.** Oil's weight among a prop's loot moves only from 25 to 60 as the lantern empties (the first try, 2026-10-10).
- **Chosen.** What the game uses.
- **+30% oil (test).** All oil that drops (jars, props, creatures) times 1.3: the owner's local test (VITE_DROP_OIL=1.3).

## What was chosen, and why

- **A broken prop gives one kind of loot, sometimes two** (gold, oil or crystals; a barrel always two), plus experience and, from crates, barrels and bone piles, now and then a piece of gear. Floors hold two crates instead of one and a half, and bone piles from floor 2. Oil, when it falls out: Clay Urn 7-11, Wooden Crate 10-16, Iron-bound Barrel 14-22, Bone Pile 5-8.
- **Oil's weight among the loot swings from 10 to 160** (beside gold 45 and crystals 15) as the lantern empties. With the first try, 25 to 60 ("Flat prop oil"), a delver with a full lantern found as much oil as one running dry, so the cheap-step builds went far deeper and the best build banked 3.7x an unperked delver's gold per cent (the economy tests allow 3.5x; it was 3.0x). The wide swing puts it back to about 3x and keeps the help for whoever runs dry.
- **Creatures drop 5-10 light** with a 35% chance when the lantern is full, rising to 80% when it is nearly empty.
- **Oil jars hold 8 light; levels give +10.**
- **`dropOil` multiplies all the oil that drops** (jars, props, creatures). It is 1 for everyone; the owner can try 1.3 in local play with `VITE_DROP_OIL=1.3` in `.env.local`.

With the chosen values 3 flasks take a newcomer to floor 4.9 on average, and 43% of 5-flask newcomers reach Cerberus. With +30% oil the same 3 flasks reach floor 5.9 and 53% of 5-flask newcomers reach the boss; a collector banks 7.22 gold per cent spent instead of 5.55, and one flask comes close to paying as well as two, which the economy tests do not allow. So +30% stays a local test until the owner decides.

## What this does not show

Bots are not people. A player who explores less, fights less, or remembers the floors will go further on the same oil; one who fights everything will go less far. The bots never buy potions (an Oil Vial is +30 light, a Healing Draught +60), which players will. The numbers show that the amount of oil is in a range where a descent can be carried on; they do not promise how it feels.
